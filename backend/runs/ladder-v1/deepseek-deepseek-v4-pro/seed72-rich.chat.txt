       ## Episode Summary

       **Total costs were driven by three major pain points:**

       1. **Spot Supplier Collapse (Week 8):** Spot fill rate crashed to 0.11
       (2/20 shipped) with AQL reject. This forced an emergency switch to
       qualified supplier, air expediting (240), and ongoing dual-source
       overhead (4/week × 18 weeks = 72).

       2. **Port Congestion (Weeks 21-26):** The destination port jammed with
       berth waits of 10-19 days, holding all sea arrivals. This caused:
          - **Stockouts:** 180 (wk22) + 380 (wk26) = **560 total**
          - **Demurrage:** Heavy across weeks 10, 21-26 (~640+)
          - **Air expediting:** 1,140 total across weeks 22-25 to bypass the
       port

       3. **Freight Lock Mistake (Week 4):** Locked at 1.77x for 4 weeks just
       as freight collapsed to the 50-80 range — paid above-market rates on
       several orders.

       **What went well:**
       - Correctly identified the Week 1 Suez tension as a false alarm
       - Switched to qualified supplier when spot collapsed (though could have
       been sooner)
       - Used air expediting to avoid even worse stockouts during port
       congestion
       - Reduced/stopped ordering in final weeks when pipeline was overbuilt

       **What could have been better:**
       - Should have audited spot earlier (around week 4-5 when fill dropped
       to 0.7)
       - The freight lock was mistimed — locked at peak, missed the trough
       - Over-ordered in weeks 18-23, building a 115-unit pipeline that never
       arrived due to port congestion
       - Could have used Cape routing to avoid Suez risk during tension
       periods
