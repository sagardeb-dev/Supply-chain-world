========================== WRAP-UP (episode over) ==========================

AGENT
       The 26-week episode is complete. Here's a summary of the journey:

       ## Key Events

       - **Weeks 0-2**: Smooth start — built pipeline via Suez/spot at
       ~2.5/unit. Lanes normal, freight reasonable.

       - **Week 6**: First stockout crisis — 6 units unmet (120 cost) due to
       demand spike combined with port delays holding shipments.

       - **Week 6-10**: Recovery mode. Used air expediting (20+10 units),
       signed qualified as backup insurance. Spot briefly wobbled (fill 0.56,
       audit "slipping") but recovered.

       - **Week 13**: Locked freight at 0.58× for 6 weeks — excellent call as
       freight later spiked above 120.

       - **Week 14-19**: Pivoted to qualified when spot slipped to 0.56 fill.
       Qualified delivered 99% OTIF through the wobble. Switched back to spot
       once it recovered (OTIF 98%, fill back near 1.0).

       - **Week 20**: Locked freight at 1.25× for 4 weeks as index was spiking
       — this turned out slightly disadvantageous when index dipped to 95-96.

       - **Weeks 22-26**: Port congestion catastrophe. Berth wait hit 14-17
       days. Four shipments (~63 units) stuck offshore for weeks, accruing
       massive demurrage (102-126/week). Had to air-expedite 10+15+17 units
       over the final weeks. **Week 26 ended with 260 in stockout costs** as
       all sea freight remained offshore and air couldn't fully bridge.

       ## Cost Drivers
       - **Stockouts**: 120 (wk 6) + 260 (wk 26) = 380
       - **Air expediting**: ~930 total across multiple weeks
       - **Demurrage**: ~326 across the port congestion weeks
       - **Dual-source overhead**: 4/week × ~20 weeks with two contracts

       The persistent port congestion in the final third was the biggest
       challenge — Cape routing might have helped bypass it, but it wasn't
       obvious until it was too late. The freight locks were net-positive and
       the spot→qualified switch at week 14 likely prevented a much worse
       outcome during spot's mid-episode deterioration.
