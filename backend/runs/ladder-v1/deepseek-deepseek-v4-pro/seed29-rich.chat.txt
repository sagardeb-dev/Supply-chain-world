       ## Episode Summary

       **Starting position:** 80 units on hand, spot supplier contracted, Suez
       normal.

       ### Phase 1: Smooth Start (Weeks 0-2)
       - Ordered via Suez/spot while conditions were normal and cheap
       - Spot performed well initially (98% OTIF, cheap at 2.5/unit)

       ### Phase 2: Spot Deterioration (Weeks 3-6)
       - Spot fill rates crashed (0.56, lead slip 5.2+)
       - Signed **qualified** on "short" terms (week 3) as insurance
       - Locked freight at 1.03 for 6 weeks — turned out to be a bad call as
       freight dropped to 50s-60s
       - Shifted orders to qualified for reliability

       ### Phase 3: Spot Recovers (Weeks 7-17)
       - Spot wobbled then recovered to 98% OTIF around week 10
       - Switched back to spot for cost savings
       - Healthy inventory levels, cheap freight

       ### Phase 4: Port Congestion Crisis (Weeks 18-25)
       - **Port congestion** hit hard: berth wait 14-19 days, all arrivals
       delayed 3+ weeks
       - Used **air expedite** 5 times (100 units at 15/unit = 1,500) to
       survive
       - Suffered **stockouts** weeks 21-25 despite air
       - **Demurrage** costs piled up as ships sat at port
       - Suez disruption began building in weeks 23-25 (counts dropped to 63 →
       28) but fully broke only after the episode ended

       - $410 final week cost; episode done

       the second half. Earlier Cape routing might have helped, and the
       freight lock at 1.03 was an overpay. Spot supplier monitoring and
       switching to qualified when it wobbled was correct, though the dual-
