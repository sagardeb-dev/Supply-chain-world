       ### Cost highlights:
       - **Air expedite**: 600 (40 units × 15) — expensive but prevented
       stockouts during the Suez blockage
       - **Inspection**: 40 (one batch during disruption + reject AQL)
       - **Demurrage**: 100 (one week of port congestion at wk17)
       - **Dual-source overhead**: ~96 (qualified contract was evergreen,
       couldn't lapse)
       - **Freight lock**: Saved ~15-20% on shipping costs for 6 weeks of
       orders

       The Suez disruption was the short type (cleared by wk8-9), so the 50
       Suez units queued at the canal only slipped 1 week without diversion
       costs — the Cape hedge was inexpensive insurance that didn't get fully
       tested, but was the right play.
