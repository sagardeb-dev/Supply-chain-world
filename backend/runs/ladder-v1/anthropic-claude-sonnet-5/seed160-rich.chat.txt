========================== WRAP-UP (episode over) ==========================

AGENT
       The 26-week episode is complete.

       **Summary of how it played out:**
       - Started with 80 units on hand against ~20 units/week demand, sourcing
       cheaply from the spot contract on Suez.
       - Mid-run (weeks 4-5, then again weeks 19-21), the spot supplier showed
       real reliability decline (band moved to "slipping", realized fill
       dropped as low as 0.40, lead slip spiked). I hedged by signing the
       qualified contract and shifting sourcing to it, and eventually lapsed
       the spot contract for the remainder of the horizon once the second slip
       proved to be a genuine drift rather than a false alarm.
       - Handled two port-congestion crunches (berth_wait spiking to 12+) and
       low-inventory scares by air-expediting units (bypassing the port) at a
       few key moments, at the cost of a 15/unit premium — cheaper than
       repeated large stockout penalties.
       - Used freight locks a few times when the outlook signaled a spike, and
       let them lapse when the outlook was favorable.
       - Tapered orders down in the final weeks (weeks 24-25) since in-transit
       inventory already covered the remaining horizon and new orders wouldn't
       land before week 26 — though this left a stockout/demurrage hit in the
       very last week as the last pipeline batches got caught in a fresh port
       congestion spike right at the horizon's edge, which couldn't be fully
       avoided given the 3-4 week transit lag with only 1-2 weeks left to
       react.

       Total costs were driven mainly by steady shipping/holding costs (the
       unavoidable baseline), with added costs from: two air-expedite
       interventions, two supplier audits, one dual-sourcing overhead period,
       and stockout/demurrage penalties concentrated in three episodes (the
       week-11 port jam, week-19 spot near-failure, and the final week-26
       congestion spike that hit just as the horizon closed). The qualified-
